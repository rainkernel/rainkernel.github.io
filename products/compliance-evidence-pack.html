The challenge

The regulators have written the record-keeping rules. The auditors are improvising the evidence.

How it works

Three steps, inside your own cloud.

  1. 01

    Record

    An SDK or an MCP proxy in your cloud writes every agent step to a log you own.

  2. 02

    Map

    Each record is mapped to the clauses your frameworks and your auditor ask for.

  3. 03

    Export

    Files and API calls into Vanta, Drata or the auditor's request folder, on a schedule or on request.

What it does

What the Compliance Evidence Pack gives you.

Tamper-evident records

Every agent step written to a hash-chained log you own, with the model, prompt and tool versions and the human approvals attached.

Continuous, not annual

The Gate's evaluations re-run on a schedule, with the results in the same log — evidence of behaviour, not a snapshot.

Exports in the shape the clause asks for

A lifetime log, decision records with explanation and human review, change and incident records, and a mapped evidence pack.

Mapped to your frameworks

ISO/IEC 42001, the EU AI Act, Colorado SB 26-189, CSA AICM and NIST AI RMF — delivered into Vanta, Drata or the auditor's request folder.
