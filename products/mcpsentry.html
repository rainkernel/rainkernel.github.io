How it works

Three steps, inside your own cloud.

  1. 01

    Scan

    The free scanner reports on any MCP server or skill set, including local ones, in minutes.

  2. 02

    Approve and pin

    What you approve is pinned; the attestation service in your cloud re-verifies it continuously.

  3. 03

    Attest

    Drift is reported with the change; a monthly attestation statement and the Agent BOM go to your risk function.

What it does

What the MCPSentry gives you.

Approval you can show

Every tool definition, server manifest and skill file your agents load is pinned at the moment you approve it.

Drift alerts

Continuous re-verification of what a server actually serves against what you approved, with the change in the alert.

Private and local servers too

Local stdio servers and internal registries, which cloud identity products cannot see — if an agent can load it, MCPSentry can watch it.

Agent BOM and permission audit

A CycloneDX-shaped inventory per agent and an over-access audit, exported for procurement questionnaires, insurers and auditors.
