How it works

Three steps, inside your own cloud.

  1. 01

    Install

    A CLI and a container in your own AWS, Azure or GCP account, or on a laptop. Nothing leaves your account.

  2. 02

    Run the Gate

    The attack pack, the harness, the cost meter, the permission audit and the BOM run against your agent and a sample of your traffic.

  3. 03

    Ship with evidence

    The Evidence Report is generated where the data lives; the CI gate re-runs it on every release and holds the line.

What it does

What the Agent Readiness Gate gives you.

Agent-specific attack pack

Attack cases mapped to the OWASP Top 10 for Agentic Applications, run against your agent and its tools — not against a generic model.

An evaluation harness from your real failures

An evaluation set seeded from your own traffic and failures, with a held-out split, that gates every later release.

Cost, permissions and the Agent BOM

Cost per completed task on your traffic, a permission and identity audit, and a CycloneDX-shaped inventory of everything the agent depends on.

One Evidence Report, three readers

Engineering gets the failing cases and the fixes, the CISO the mapped findings, audit the inventory and the controls statement — regenerated on every release.
