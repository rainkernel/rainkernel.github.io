Privacy notice

Privacy notice

Effective 27 September 2026 · v2.0

Rainkernel Technologies Private Limited ("Rainkernel", "we") is an AI engineering company registered in Hyderabad, India. This notice explains what we collect through rainkernel.com and in the course of our work, and how we handle it.

What this site collects

The site sets no cookies, runs no analytics scripts and loads nothing from third parties. Two forms send data to our own servers, hosted on Amazon Web Services in the Mumbai (ap-south-1) region:

  • Contact form — your name, work email, company (optional) and message, plus the time and a network address hash used only to stop abuse. We use it to reply to you.
  • Readiness Score "email me this result" — your email and your eight answers, so we can send the result and, if you ask, follow up.

The score itself is computed in your browser; nothing is sent unless you use the email option. Submissions are kept for 24 months and then deleted, unless an engagement follows or you ask us to delete them sooner. Request logs at our content-delivery edge are kept for 30 days.

Email and booking

If you email us or book a call, we hold your name, email address, company and the content of the conversation for as long as it lasts, and for up to 24 months afterwards for follow-up.

Client data during engagements

During a sprint or build, client data stays in the client's environment. We do not train models on client data, we do not retain copies after an engagement ends except as the statement of work requires, and we delete working copies within 30 days of a written request. Access is limited to named engineers under a non-disclosure agreement.

Your rights

You may ask what we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, or withdraw consent, by writing to privacy@rainkernel.com. We respond within 30 days. We process personal data under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and, for clients in the EU or UK, under the GDPR and UK GDPR as a processor acting on the client's instructions.

Security

Report a security issue to security@rainkernel.com; our disclosure details are in security.txt.

Changes

We will post any change to this notice on this page with a new effective date.
