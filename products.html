The shared design

What every Rainkernel product has in common.

  • Inside your cloud

    Every product installs in your own account. The evidence never leaves it.

  • A published price

    Every tier is on the pricing page, including the products still in build.

  • One report, three readers

    Engineer, CISO and auditor read the same artefact, mapped to the frameworks they already use.

  • Open frame, licensed engine

    The Readiness Kit is open source; the attack packs, report generator and gates are the licence.
