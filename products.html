The shared design

What every Rainkernel product has in common

  • An artefact for four readers

    Engineer, CISO, auditor, insurer — one report, written for all of them, with the findings mapped to the frameworks they already use.

  • Inside your cloud

    Engines, gateways and recorders run in your estate. The evidence never leaves it; 77% of leaders weigh a vendor's country of origin, so we built sovereignty in.

  • A published price

    No funded vendor in this market discloses one. We publish every tier and quote within it.

  • A rung of the ladder

    Each product makes the sprint, the build, the retainer or the pod worth more — and each is sold inside them as well as alone.

  • Open engines, closed loop

    Built on open-source scanners and gateways under our own corpus, rubric and report generator, which we also publish.

  • Honest exits

    Knowledge transfer, IP rights, defined human–AI decision rights and a planned exit in every statement of work.
