The call

What happens on a 30-minute call.

How long is it, and what do I bring?

Thirty minutes, no deck. Bring the agent or the idea, the data it reads, the systems it acts on and the number it has to hit.

Do I need an agent in production?

No. A pilot that stalled is the usual starting point; a design on paper is fine too.

What do I leave with?

A read on where the agent would stall, and, if it fits, an early-access date and an order form inside the published price within 48 hours.

What if it does not fit?

We say so on the call and, where we can, point you somewhere better. Nothing is sold on the call.
