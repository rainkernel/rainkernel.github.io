How pricing works

Three rules.

  • The page is the ceiling

    A quote never exceeds the published price. If a price changes, the page changes first; existing licences keep theirs until renewal.

  • Founding and pilot prices are locked

    A founding Gate licence keeps 60% of list for two years; a Governor pilot keeps its price for twelve months; a design partner keeps $6,000 a year for two years.

  • Your cloud, your bill

    Every product installs in your accounts. Model and cloud usage stays on your invoices; we never meter your traffic. USD by wire, or INR with 18% GST at the rupee figure on the tier.

FAQ

Questions about pricing

Are these the real prices or starting points for a negotiation?+

They are the prices. A quote never exceeds the figure on this page; where a range is published, the exact figure is in the order form and sits inside the range.

Is anything delivered by your engineers?+

Installation help on a shared channel for every early-access and pilot customer, and support at the level of your tier. We do not sell engineering time: the report tells your team what to fix, and the Gate re-runs when it is fixed.

What currency are we invoiced in, and what about tax?+

International customers in USD by SWIFT wire against export invoices; Indian customers in INR at the rupee figure on the tier, plus 18% GST. Prices exclude taxes.

What happens if a product in build slips?+

The window on its page moves and says so the same day. A founding Gate licence is refunded in full if early access is not delivered by 15 December 2026; a pilot or design-partner order is not invoiced until the software is installed.

Do you offer discounts for startups, non-profits or long commitments?+

The founding, pilot and design-partner prices are the discounts, with their conditions on this page. Beyond those, the published price is the price.

How do payment terms work?+

Annual licences are invoiced at signing, payable in 15 days; monthly licences monthly in advance. The order form and the licence agreement set out the rest and never change the price on this page.

Want a founding licence, or the exact figure for your estate?

Tell us the agent, the estate and the deadline. The order form arrives within 48 hours of the call, inside the published price, with the early-access date on it.
