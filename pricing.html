How pricing works

Six rules we hold ourselves to.

  • The page is the ceiling

    A quote never exceeds the published price or range. If we have to change a price, the page changes first and the date is noted.

  • Fixed means fixed

    Sprints, Gates, audits and builds are fixed-price for the scope in the statement of work. Scope creep is a conversation, not an invoice.

  • The sprint pays for itself

    The $7,500 sprint fee is credited in full against a Build to Production signed within 30 days of the readout.

  • Your cloud, your bill

    Engines, gateways and recorders run in your accounts. Model and cloud usage stays on your invoices, where your finance team can see it.

  • Two currencies, one price

    USD by wire for international clients; INR with 18% GST for Indian clients at the rupee figure shown, not a converted one.

  • No surprises at renewal

    Retainers and attestation are monthly with a three-month minimum. The exit terms — knowledge transfer, IP, decision rights — are written into every statement of work.

FAQ

Questions about pricing

Are these the real prices or starting points for a negotiation?+

They are the prices. A quote never exceeds the figure on this page; where a range is published, the exact figure is in the proposal and sits inside the range. If a price changes, it changes here first.

What currency are we invoiced in, and what about tax?+

International clients are invoiced in USD by SWIFT wire against export invoices. Indian clients are invoiced in INR with 18% GST added. Prices on this page exclude taxes.

Is the sprint fee credited against a build?+

Yes. The $7,500 sprint fee is credited in full against a Build to Production signed within 30 days of the readout.

What is included in a fixed price, and what is not?+

Every deliverable listed on the service or product page, the readout, and a 30-day warranty on builds. Not included: your cloud and model-provider usage (it runs in your accounts), travel if you ask for on-site days, and net-new use cases, which are scoped as another sprint.

Do you offer discounts for startups, non-profits or long commitments?+

We do not run a discount list; the published price is the price. Longer commitments are quoted inside the published band, never below the page.

How do payment terms work?+

Terms are set out in the statement of work for each engagement; they never change the price on this page.

Want the exact figure for your estate?

Tell us the agent, the estate and the deadline; the fixed-price proposal arrives within 48 hours of the call, inside the published range.
