Careers

We hire against signed work. Here is the plan.

Rainkernel is founder-led and self-funded, so there is no hiring ahead of revenue and no open role we cannot pay for twice over. The roles below open in the order the work arrives. If one of them is you, write now — the list is short and we read it first.

Write to hello@rainkernel.comOur values

The first roles

No open roles today. These open first.

  • Senior product engineer

    Opens when five founding Gate licences are signed or the backlog reaches eight engineer-weeks — planned Q1 2027

    Owns the Gate's report generator, the CI gate and the Governor's policy engine; runs early-access installs with the founder. Python or TypeScript, one cloud deeply, a record of shipping something that stayed up.

  • Agent security engineer (two roles)

    Open after Gate general availability — planned Q1–Q2 2027

    Writes the attack packs and the domain packs, keeps the Readiness Kit honest, triages what MCPSentry's scanner finds. Two to five years of production software; no AI title required, curiosity about why pilots stall is.

  • Solutions engineer

    Opens with the first partner licences — planned Q2 2027

    Installs the products in customers' and partners' clouds, answers the technical questions on every call, writes the runbooks. Customer-facing from week one; same bar, same tools.

What it is like

Small, technical, on the record.

Where
Hyderabad; the products run in customers' clouds, so travel is rare and planned.
Hours
Monday to Friday, 09:00–21:00 IST (overlaps US East mornings, UK and EU afternoons). Calls with US customers sit in the evening; mornings are for building.
Pay
Hyderabad market rate, stated in the offer; a hire is made only when two months of that salary are already in the company account — the rule that keeps the company solvent and the job real.
Kit
The open-source engines we build on, the Readiness Kit, and a customer install to prove yourself in from the first month.
What you get to say
Engineers run customer calls and publish in the Lab's weekly log under their own name. Credit is public.

What we look for

The values, applied to hiring.

  • Evidence over opinion — Every claim on this site has its source one click away, and every deliverable we hand over is a measurement, not a slide. If we cannot put a number on it, we do not sell it.
  • Published prices — Nobody in this market publishes a price. We do, for every product and every tier, and a quote never exceeds the price on the page.
  • Your cloud, your data, your harness — Every product installs inside your estate. Evaluation sets, reports, logs and BOMs are files in open formats in your account; we train nothing on customer data and we hold no copies.
  • Products, not projects — We sell software and support, never engineering time. The report tells your team what to fix, in priority order; the Gate re-runs when it is fixed. A company that sold the fixing would have a reason to find more of it.

The values above are the ones we hire against; the rest is on the about page.

How to apply

One email, three things.

  1. Something you built that ran in production, with a link or a paragraph on what broke and what you did about it.
  2. Which of the roles above, and when you could start.
  3. One question about the line-up on this site that you think we have got wrong. We will answer it.

Send it to hello@rainkernel.com with the subject "Careers". Every application gets a reply, including the no's. Agencies: please don't.
