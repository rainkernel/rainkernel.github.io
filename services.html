The sprint, day by day

Ten working days, one readout, no surprises.

The clock starts when we have data access, not when the SOW is signed. The deliverable is the Agent Readiness Gate's Evidence Report.

  1. Days 1–2

    Scope and access

    One use case with a measurable target; data access and environment set up; the evaluation cases agreed.

  2. Days 3–7

    Thin slice

    A working agent on your data, evaluated daily against the case set; cost per task measured from day one; the attack pack run.

  3. Days 8–9

    Evidence Report

    The 8-area readiness scorecard, the Gate's findings mapped to OWASP and NIST, the gaps that would stall production, and the fixed-price plan to close them.

  4. Day 10

    Readout

    Sixty minutes with your decision-makers: the demo on your data, the score, the plan, the price. You decide.

How we work

Five rules in every statement of work.

  1. 01
    Scope in numbers

    One use case, one measurable target, one data source, one channel. The acceptance criteria are agreed before the clock starts.

  2. 02
    Inside your cloud

    Access to your environment, not copies of your data. Engines and reports run and stay where your data already lives.

  3. 03
    Evaluate from day one

    A real-case evaluation set and a cost-per-task meter exist before the first prompt is tuned. Every change is scored against them.

  4. 04
    Readout, not report-out

    Sixty minutes with the decision-makers: the demo on your data, the score, the gaps, the fixed-price plan. You decide.

  5. 05
    Hand over, every time

    Harness, runbook, training and the right to walk away are in every SOW. Retainers earn renewal; they do not hold you.

FAQ

Questions about working with us

Why does every engagement start with the sprint?+

Because in two weeks you see your own agent evaluated on your own data, with a cost number and a mapped findings list, before committing to anything larger. The sprint fee is credited against a Build signed within 30 days, so nobody pays twice for the same scoping.

Who owns the harness and the code?+

You do, on full payment, as every statement of work says. Rainkernel keeps its pre-existing tools and the open-source Readiness Kit, licensed to you as embedded.

Do you work on-site?+

Yes, in Hyderabad the same week; elsewhere by arrangement. Most engagements run remotely inside your cloud with daily written updates and a weekly call.

How do you invoice internationally?+

In USD, by wire or card, from Rainkernel Technologies Private Limited in India. Indian clients are invoiced in INR with GST. Payment terms are in the order; the sprint is paid on signature.

What if the sprint shows the use case is not ready for an agent at all?+

Then the readout says so, with the reasons and the numbers, and the recommendation might be a workflow change or a smaller automation. We would rather lose a Build than deliver one that stalls.
