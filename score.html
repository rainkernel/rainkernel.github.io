How production-ready is your agent?

Eight questions from the sprint scorecard. Answer honestly — partly is a valid answer — and you get a score out of 100, the two areas most likely to stall in production, and what the sprint would do about each. The result lives in the page address, so you can send it to a colleague.

0 of 8 answered

  1. 01Evaluation setDo you have a set of real cases with expected outcomes that every change is scored against?
  2. 02GuardrailsAre prompt-injection, data-leak and off-policy outputs tested, not just described?
  3. 03Cost per taskDo you know the cost per completed task today, and the ceiling at production volume?
  4. 04ObservabilityCan you see every step of a run — retrieval, tool calls, model output — with latency and errors?
  5. 05Data accessDoes the agent read production data through governed, permissioned access rather than exports?
  6. 06Human-in-the-loopIs there a defined point where a person approves, corrects or takes over, and is that path exercised?
  7. 07ReliabilityAre retries, timeouts, fallbacks and idempotency handled for every tool the agent calls?
