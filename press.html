Boilerplate

Describe us accurately.

One line

Rainkernel builds the production layer AI agents are missing — evaluation, cost governance, tool attestation and audit-grade records — inside clients' own clouds, at published prices.

One paragraph

Rainkernel Technologies Private Limited is a product company founded in Hyderabad, India, in September 2026. Its four products — the Agent Readiness Gate, the Agent Cost Governor, MCPSentry and the Compliance Evidence Pack — test AI agents before they go live, govern what they cost to run, verify the tools they use and produce the records auditors and regulators ask for. Each is software customers install in their own cloud under a licence with a published price; the engine under the Gate, the Readiness Kit, is open source. Rainkernel publishes its prices, its evaluation tooling and a weekly log of checkable work, and is recognised as a startup by India's Department for Promotion of Industry and Internal Trade.

Name: "Rainkernel" (one word, capital R). Legal name: Rainkernel Technologies Private Limited. Product names: Agent Readiness Gate, Agent Cost Governor, MCPSentry (one word, capital S), Compliance Evidence Pack; the open-source Readiness Kit. MCPSentry is a Rainkernel product, not a separate company. Rainkernel sells software licences only; it has no services arm.
