How it works

Ten working days, one readout, no surprises.

The clock starts when we have data access, not when the SOW is signed.

  1. Days 1–2

    Scope and access

    One use case with a measurable target; data access and environment set up; the evaluation cases agreed.

  2. Days 3–7

    Thin slice

    A working agent on your data, evaluated daily against the case set; cost per task measured from day one.

  3. Days 8–9

    Scorecard

    The 8-area readiness scorecard, the gaps that would stall production, and the fixed-price plan to close them.

  4. Day 10

    Readout

    Sixty minutes with your decision-makers: the demo on your data, the score, the plan, the price. You decide.

Product

Rainkernel Readiness Kit — the production gate for AI agents

The evaluation harness, agent attack packs (OWASP Agentic Top 10), cost and latency budgets and the observability template we run inside every sprint — packaged so any team can score an agent before it ships. The result is a signed readiness scorecard across eight areas, mapped to the EU AI Act, ISO/IEC 42001 and the NIST AI RMF.
