Questions

Straight answers.

Do I own it?

Yes. The code lives in your own GitHub organisation from day one, the domain stays in your name, and there is nothing to migrate if you leave.

Why is it cheaper than an agency?

AI writes the first draft of the copy and the code in hours; an engineer spends the rest of the five days on the part AI skips — tests, accessibility, security, performance. You pay for the second part.

What is not included?

Online shops, logins, custom back-ends and multilingual sites are quoted separately after the intake call. Photography and video are yours to supply, or we use licensed stock.

Can you host it?

Hosting costs ₹0 on GitHub Pages or CloudFront; the care plan keeps it running, changes content within two working days and reports monthly.

How do I pay?

Indian clients: 50% advance by UPI or bank transfer against a GST invoice, balance on launch. Overseas clients: card or bank transfer in USD, invoiced under our export registration.
